# PromptFlowAS

![PromptFlowAS Logo](public/logos/logo.svg)

**Discover better prompts. Learn practical AI skills. Build smarter workflows.**

PromptFlowAS is an open-source library of practical AI prompts, skills, and workflows designed to help people get more useful results from AI.

Instead of starting every task from a blank chat, PromptFlowAS gives you ready-to-use resources that can be explored, customized, copied, and launched in your preferred AI assistant.

---

## ✨ What is PromptFlowAS?

AI is becoming part of everyday work, learning, creativity, research, business, coding, and decision-making.

But knowing **what to ask** can be just as important as knowing **which AI tool to use**.

PromptFlowAS helps make that easier.

You can explore:

- 🧠 **Prompts** — Ready-to-use prompts for real-world tasks
- 🛠️ **Skills** — Practical AI skills worth learning
- 🔄 **Workflows** — Step-by-step ways to use AI for larger tasks
- 📚 **Guides** — Learn how to work more effectively with AI
- 🔎 **Related Resources** — Discover connected prompts, skills, and workflows

---

## 🚀 Use a Prompt

Find a prompt that matches what you want to accomplish.

Customize its variables if needed, then:

- **Copy the prompt**
- **Launch it directly in ChatGPT**
- **Launch it in Claude**
- **Launch it in Gemini**
- **Launch it in Grok**
- Or copy the prompt and use it with another AI assistant of your choice

You are never locked into one AI platform.

---

## 🌍 Use AI in Your Language

AI should not require everyone to communicate in the same language.

When using a PromptFlowAS prompt, you can simply tell your preferred AI assistant:

> "Please respond in my language: [your language]."

For example:

> "Explain this in Assamese."

or

> "Please answer in Hindi."

or

> "Respond in my language: Bengali."

This makes the same prompt useful for people across different languages and backgrounds.

## 🧩 Explore More

PromptFlowAS is designed for exploration.

When you finish using one resource, you can discover related:

**Prompts → Skills → Workflows**

For example:

**Learn a concept → Find a learning prompt → Explore a study skill → Discover a study workflow**

The goal is not simply to give you one prompt.

The goal is to help you discover **better ways to work with AI**.
